## Purpose

nicosadia.me is a my own website to collect my projects, notes and other things I want to share.
I intend the site to be a bit more personal than professional as I want to share this site with friends, family, etc.
My goal is to regularly maintain and update this website, so stay tuned!

## Technical stuff

This site is built with Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS.  
Project write-ups use MDX and reusable components for content such as diagrams and screenshots, with images served from a Cloudflare R2 bucket.
